🌡️ Evie warm-up calibration

Evie's warm-ups are timed from the weather forecast for each departure, and every trip teaches the bot how long she really takes, so the start time gets closer to “ready right at departure”.

  • 19 °C cabin when it's below 15 °C outside
  • 21 °C between 15 and 25 °C
  • 20.5 °C (cool-down) above 25 °C

22 completed warm-ups so far, all with 5 cm of snow or less. The dashed blue line is a refit of cabin time-to-target vs outside temperature from 9 sessions with a trustworthy reached-target time (ordinary least squares, y = 7.5 + 0.17×T minutes, R² = 0.10, RMSE = 2.93 min, the slope is not statistically significant, p = 0.42). The shaded band is a 95% prediction interval. Sessions used for the fit span -7.1 to 10.1 °C; outside that range the line is an extrapolation. Defrost sessions with a cabin-sensor spike or a climate-off overshoot cut were left out of the fit (they still appear as points). This fitted line is for the chart only; the scheduler on the mini still uses its rule table, frost/snow adders, and its own learning. The dashed red line is the fitted no-snow curve plus a 15-minute snow allowance (the scheduler’s adder for 10 cm of normal snow, 5–15 cm tier). It is not yet fitted from observations: there are no completed sessions with more than 5 cm of snow. The red band is the same 95% prediction interval shifted up by that allowance.

The dashed blue line is a linear fit of observed cabin time-to-target vs outside temperature (not the scheduler’s planned lead, which also adds frost/snow time). The dashed red line is that same fit plus the scheduler’s snow allowance for 10 cm of normal snow; it is not yet fitted (no sessions with more than 5 cm of snow).
